About

Your peaceful base in South Bali

Welcome to Sari Hills, your comfortable base in South Bali. Located between Nusa Dua and Uluwatu, our guesthouse is ideally placed for exploring some of the island’s most popular beaches and attractions. We offer five private rooms, each with its own bathroom, in a relaxed and friendly setting.

Spend your days by the shared pool, on the patio, or in the covered outdoor kitchen. Oasis Padel is just 4 minutes away, and top beach clubs like Savaya, El Kabron, Single Fin, and Sundays Beach Club are close by.
